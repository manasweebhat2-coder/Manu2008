The Soul of the Sketch

A handcrafted studio for personal portrait narratives.

In an age of instant digital replication, Namachitra is a return to the intentional. Born from a quiet obsession with paper, ink, and storytelling — we create bespoke, hand-sketched name boards that serve as physical mirrors of your life's journey.

Every piece is a unique artifact designed to evoke reflection in a hyper-digitized world. Each commission begins with listening deeply, interpreting hidden motifs, and translating them into a crafted portrait that lives on your wall.

Tangibility

Reflection

Authenticity

Founder
Iris Navarro

Artist, storyteller, and stationery obsessive. I created Namachitra to honor personal histories through handcrafted portraiture — one story, one ink stroke, one framed memory at a time.

This idea grew from the desire to transform names and life moments into tangible, heirloom art — as intimate as a letter pressed into thick stationery.

Our Tenet

"We believe art should do more than decorate; it should motivate."

By illustrating your hidden motifs and specific milestones, we transform a simple name into a source of intrinsic motivation — a daily visual anchor that honors your unique rhythm and ambitions.

01
Tangibility Over Pixels

Real ink on real paper carries a weight that no screen can replicate. Our work exists in the physical world — to be touched, framed, and passed down.

02
Reflection as Ritual

Art placed in your daily line of sight becomes a ritual. Our portraits are designed to be daily triggers for self-realization in a hyper-digitized world.

03
Authentic Over Algorithmic

Every stroke is a human decision. We are a deliberate rejection of mass-produced, AI-generated aesthetics — proof that the hand still matters.

Mission

To provide every individual with a handcrafted visual anchor that honors their unique rhythm and ambitions.

Every commission is an act of listening. We capture what you cannot always articulate and translate it into something you can hang on your wall and return to, daily.

Vision

To lead a global movement toward personalized, reflective art that prioritizes human storytelling over digital convenience.

In a world increasingly shaped by algorithms, we believe the handmade is an act of resistance — and a gift of permanence to oneself.

The Process

Our meticulous four-step process ensures every portrait is a masterpiece worthy of your story.

I
Step One
The Discovery Form

Tell us your story — your name, heritage, milestones, values, and vision. This intimate questionnaire is where we truly begin to listen.

II
Step Two
The Curation Call

A personal consultation with our lead artist to refine your vision and concept. We interpret your hidden motifs and agree on the composition.

III
Step Three
Hand-Sketching

Our artists meticulously hand-craft your portrait. Five to ten days of pure artistry — every stroke intentional, every element earned.

IV
Step Four
Hand-Delivery

Your framed masterpiece arrives carefully packaged, ready to hang on your wall and anchor your days for years to come.
